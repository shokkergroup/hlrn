## Full-broadcast race layer

- 351 primary-broadcast chapters across 20 official races.
- 15–18 chronological cuts per race.
- Every cut stays on that race’s own full upload.
- Every cut has a start, bounded end, category, phase, title, summary, source
  ID, claim state, and stable wiki route.
- Clicking a cut seeks the persistent embedded race player and updates the URL.
- Previous/next controls move through the full race reel.
- **Copy Wiki Cut** produces an exact in-wiki permalink; the adjacent link opens
  the same second on YouTube.
- **Copy Race Index** exports every primary-broadcast cut with in/out time,
  exact wiki route, and result/editor/transcript review boundary.
- A race-signal filter isolates restarts, stages, incidents, strategy, battles,
  booth calls, finishes, and results.
- All 20 opening greens and live-race closes are manually reviewed.
- 34 later replay/recap windows are labeled post-race.
- 118 navigation chapters were individually corrected or reviewed against the
  primary tape.
- 208 remaining bounded navigation cues have local primary captions inside the
  playback window; every card that tags a driver re-resolves at least one
  identity through the alias registry in that same window.
- 13 primary result receipts synchronize to the exact chapter second.

The chapter gate rejects cross-source playback, duplicate titles within a race,
duplicate summaries, raw caption noise, invalid time windows, chronology
errors, missing race-envelope anchors, unregistered drivers, and claim-state
mismatches.
